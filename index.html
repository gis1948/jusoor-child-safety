Jusoor Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

Jusoor is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all members of the Jusoor community.

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Jusoor strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and any content, activity, or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

Jusoor does not allow:

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation of any kind.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including the creation, upload, sharing, distribution, solicitation, or promotion of such material.
  • Sexualization of children or minors.
  • Sexual grooming or attempts to establish sexual contact with a child.
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, or solicitation of children.
  • Using Jusoor to facilitate, arrange, promote, or encourage sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Requests for, offers of, or attempts to obtain sexual content involving children.
  • Any other conduct that violates applicable child-safety or child-protection laws.

These standards apply to all users, content, communications, profiles, and activities associated with Jusoor.

2. Reporting Child-Safety Concerns

Anyone who becomes aware of suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, CSAM, grooming, or another serious child-safety concern involving Jusoor should report it immediately.

When reporting a concern, please provide as much relevant information as possible, including the username or profile involved, a description of the concern, and links or other identifying information when available.

Do not send or redistribute suspected CSAM. If possible, provide identifying information or a link that allows the Jusoor Child Safety Team to locate the reported material.

3. Action on Violations

Jusoor may take appropriate action when it identifies or receives a report of activity that violates these Child Safety Standards.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, actions may include:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Restricting, suspending, or terminating accounts.
  • Blocking users from further interaction.
  • Preserving relevant information when appropriate and legally permitted.
  • Reporting suspected CSAM or child sexual exploitation to appropriate authorities or organizations when required or appropriate.

Jusoor may cooperate with law enforcement and relevant child-protection organizations in accordance with applicable law.

5. Compliance

These Child Safety Standards are part of the rules governing use of Jusoor. Jusoor may update these standards when necessary to address changes in applicable law, platform requirements, or child-safety practices.

Jusoor is committed to maintaining a safe community and to preventing the use of its services for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation.
